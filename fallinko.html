Fallinko

In Active Development Physics Arcade Kinetic Pegs & Drops Unity 3D / 2D

A physics-driven casual arcade game combining kinetic pinball-inspired ball drops, bouncing peg boards, multiplier collection buckets, and satisfying upgrade progression.

Development in Progress Inquire for Beta Testing

Core Concepts & Planned Mechanics

Kinetic Ball Physics

Accurate, hyper-satisfying rigid-body collisions, realistic ball weights, and bouncy obstacles delivering unpredictable kinetic cascades with every drop.

Dynamic Multiplier Buckets

Direct balls into high-multiplier bottom buckets with moving hazards, bonus bumpers, and slot accelerators to maximize level high scores.

Upgrade & Progression Tree

Reinvest points to upgrade ball capacity, bounce elasticity, magnet attractors, and special elemental orb effects.

Handcrafted & Algorithmic Stages

A blend of structured puzzle levels requiring precise drop angles and endless procedural stages for arcade score chasing.
