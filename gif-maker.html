GIF Maker: Video to GIF

GIF Converter Jetpack Compose No Watermark Closed Testing on Google Play

A high-speed, lightweight, and offline Android GIF creator built with Jetpack Compose. Convert video clips to crisp animated GIFs, create animations from photos, convert GIFs back to MP4 videos, and extract pinpoint still frames—100% on-device with zero watermarks.

GET IT ON Google Play App Privacy Policy Support & Feedback

Key Features & Capabilities

Video to GIF Conversion

Convert any MP4, WEBM, or MOV video into smooth GIFs. Adjust target FPS (5 to 30 FPS), output resolution, playback speed (0.25x to 4x), and reversible boomerang bounce loops.

Photos to Animated GIF

Combine multiple photos and screenshots into seamless GIF slideshows or stop-motion animations. Customize frame durations, ordering, and transition speeds.

GIF to MP4 Video

Convert existing GIF files back to MP4 videos optimized for Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp with configurable loop repetition counts (1x, 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x).

Precision Frame Extraction

Scrub through any video or GIF frame-by-frame. Extract individual high-resolution PNG/JPG stills at the exact millisecond you need.

100% On-Device & Private

All video encoding and rendering runs entirely on your phone via Android MediaCodec hardware pipelines. No cloud uploads, no account registration, zero tracking.

Zero Watermarks & Unlimited Exports

Every GIF and video export is completely watermark-free, giving you full creative ownership over your media content without hidden restrictions.
