Triple Tiles: Face Match Puzzle

Match-3 Puzzle Casual Offline Play Live on Google Play

An engaging, calming, and deeply tactile match-3 puzzle game where players match expressive face tiles, solve layered board formations, and unlock rich customization themes.

Get it on Google Play Game Privacy Policy Player Support

Core Highlights & Gameplay Features

Expressive Face Tile Mechanics

Unlike standard generic tiles, each tile features personality-rich emotive faces and character illustrations that make clearing clusters intuitively recognizable and visually rewarding.

Layered 3D Tile Board Formations

Tackle intricate 3D stacked board layouts. Strategically reveal covered tiles, manage your 7-slot holding bar, and clear layers without overflowing your tray.

Tactile Haptic Feedback

Designed with customized vibration impulses on tile taps, triple-match clears, and victory animations for a tactile, satisfying sensory experience.

100% Offline Playability

No continuous internet connection required. Play on commutes, flights, or off-grid without interruptions or annoying network lockouts.

Unlockable Skin & Theme Packs

Progress through levels and complete daily challenges to unlock fresh tile sets including animal faces, retro pixel art, and seasonal event skins.

Smooth 60 FPS Performance

Optimized with lightweight shaders, batch-rendered UI, and low-power Unity engine pipelines ensuring silky smooth animations on devices of all tiers.
