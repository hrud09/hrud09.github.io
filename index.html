Clipr: Quick Video Tools

Video Editor Jetpack Compose On-Device AI 100% Privacy-First

A lightweight, privacy-focused mobile video editing studio built with Jetpack Compose, Media3/ExoPlayer, and Google ML Kit. Seamlessly cut, trim, and merge video clips, apply smart on-device subject segmentation, inject custom intro frames, and overlay background audio tracks—all computed 100% locally on your device with zero data tracking.

  • Precision video trimming, cutting, and multi-clip merging
  • On-device AI subject segmentation powered by Google ML Kit
  • Custom opening image frames & background audio mixing
  • 100% local processing with offline Room draft project management
